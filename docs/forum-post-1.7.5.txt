IL-2 Korea Service Record 1.7.5 — what you are working towards, and a case to put it in


Gentlemen,

1.7.5 is mostly artwork. One new thing: the record now tells you what your pilot is
working towards, and how far off it is.


WHAT COMES NEXT

Under the awards on a pilot's page there is now a short list: for every ladder he is
already on, the next rung and the distance to it. Not a catalogue of everything in the
game — only the things actually in front of him.

Most rungs are a number, and the bar says which number:

    Bronze Star Medal                     16 / 35 sorties
    Legion of Merit                     15.8 / 200 hours
    Distinguished Unit Citation        1,551 / 14,000 targets
    Promotion to Lieutenant Colonel       35 / 75 days · 229.8 / 500 points

Some are not a number at all. The Purple Heart wants a wound, so it says "Wounded in
Action" rather than pretending you are a percentage of the way there. Others want a
rank, or squadron command, and say so.

Plenty can be earned more than one way, and that is the rule rather than the exception
outside the American set — of the Soviet and Korean decorations, five routes to the same
medal is ordinary. The nearest one is the bar; the others fold away under a chevron,
each showing its own progress. So the Order of the Red Star may head with nine sorties
of the fifteen it wants, and open to show that thirty ground targets or three air
victories would have done as well.

Three things to know about it before you rely on it:

  * It reads the same conditions the game does, out of awards.cfg. If you run the awards
    mod, it reads your modded file, so the figures are yours and not mine.
  * Where a condition rolls dice, it says the odds per mission rather than a date. That
    is the honest answer, not a hedge.
  * It only shows the ladders a pilot is already on. A decoration he has never touched
    does not appear, because the engine will not consider him for it either.

One finding while building it, which may save someone else the puzzling. Some awards in
the file have a second route written as AwardByDef. Reading it, that route looks live —
a coin-flip on every sortie. It is not. Across a full career I could find no pilot who
had ever received an award by it, for any of the decorations that offer it. Something
gates it that I have not identified, so the list ignores that route entirely. If it
ever fires for you, I would like to know.


THE DISPLAY CASE

There is a new button under the awards: a glazed case with the pilot's decorations
mounted in it — medals, his badge, unit citations and squadron insignia, with his rank
and name engraved on the brass.

Three of them, for the Air Force, the Soviets and the North Koreans. The eastern cases
are not the American one recoloured. Soviet awards divide by how they are worn rather
than by seniority alone — the orders that hang from a ribbon in one row, the screw-back
orders that have no ribbon at all in another, the Gold Star above them — so those cases
are built that way.

Each plate is engraved in the language of the air force that issued the awards, not in
yours. A Soviet case reads Майор whatever language you have the page set to; hover the
brass and it tells you the rank in your own. The name is never touched, whatever it is.

The one air force this cannot serve properly is the North Korean. The game ships no
Korean strings at all — its Chinese for the 503rd is 少校, which is the Chinese rank and
wrong on a Korean plate — but its English is not "Major" either, it is "Sojwa", the
Korean rank romanised. So that is what a North Korean plate carries: the right word in
the wrong alphabet, which is the best the game has to offer.

Nothing for the Navy or the Marines yet. The artwork is drawn and sitting in the
release, but the game has no career for either service, so there is nothing to put in a
case and no way for me to check one.


SERVICE DRESS

The uniform view now shows the right coat for the occasion. Service dress for the Air
Force is the silver-tan; for the Navy, khaki. Blue and white are full dress, which is
what carries the full-size medals.

Each wears its own rank. The Navy's khaki shows it twice, on the shoulder boards and
again on the shirt collar, as it was worn.


MEDALS AND RIBBONS

All seventeen full-size medals are redrawn. They are also no longer wearing two shadows
each: thirteen of them had one painted into the picture on top of the one the coat
casts, which is a good part of why the rack used to look uneven from medal to medal.

Ribbons and drapes are lit now rather than flat. The cloth bows over its mount the way
it does in life, brightest a little above centre and falling away to the edges. The
weave is untouched — it is only the light that is new.


THREE FIXES WORTH KNOWING

If you have been looking at a medal in the tracker and thinking it did not match the one
in the game, that was real and it was mine. Award pictures were being cached in your
browser for a year against an address that never changed, so a decoration you remodelled
never reached the page. Fixed properly: the address now moves when the atlas does. If
you have modded award art, it should finally show what you drew.

The Career Helper's Revive tab has a way back for your own pilot. Its Captured tab
will always mark him missing - that is the point of it - but Revive used to list only
your squadron mates, so marking your own man was a one-way door, and one of you walked
through it. He is on the list now.

The window also widens from the day of the loss to that day and the one after, for your
pilot and your squadron mates alike. That mistake is nearly always noticed one click too
late, with End Day already pressed. One day and no more, though: a man brought back a
week on walks into a squadron whose war went without him, days of sorties he was not on
and losses he never saw. If you are past the window, your last backup is the way back -
the Helper writes one before every change it makes.

And the Career Helper no longer offers awards for withdrawal as "#188 pilot 20 award
601014". It names the man and the decoration.


THE MOD

The awards mod is unchanged from 1.7.0, byte for byte. Nothing to merge or redo.
