IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.3.0 — personnel files for every air force, Chinese merits, updates from inside the Helper


Gentlemen,

2.3.0 is out. The personnel file now exists for every air force you can
fly, the Chinese pilots finally have decorations worth earning, and from
this version on the Career Helper can update the whole package itself.

UPDATES FROM INSIDE THE CAREER HELPER

- A new tab, "Update IL-2 Korea Service Record", shows your version and
  checks for a newer release when you ask it to - never by itself.
- One click downloads the new release, checks it and installs it: the
  tracker, the Helper and the awards mod in the game folder, keeping your
  folders and your promotion choice. Close the game first.
- Nothing is installed unless the download matches GitHub's checksum and
  carries the same signature as the program you are running.
- This version still has to be installed by hand. From here on, the
  Helper does it.

NEW: PERSONNEL FILES FOR THE PRC, THE USSR AND NORTH KOREA

Each file is written in its own air force's language, as it would have
been kept. Point at any line and it is translated into yours.

- Chinese pilots: a cadre record, the merit booklet with one entry for
  every merit, the Air Force certificate for First and Special Class
  merit (after Hua Longyi's of 1951) and a page for the Combat Hero.
- Soviet pilots: the personal file, record of service, the commander's
  attestation, the order extracts for each promotion, the order booklet,
  the Hero of the Soviet Union certificate and the nomination sheets.
- North Korean pilots: the cadre record, an order booklet for each order,
  the Presidium's certificate for a Hero, and the biography in Korean.
- Navy and Marine Corps pilots get the U.S. file with their own
  commission, the Navy's Aviator's Flight Log Book and the Navy's own
  certificates - ready for the day the game gives us naval squadrons.

AWARDS MOD

- Chinese pilots earn merit for air victories in one sortie: Third Class
  from 2, Second Class from 3, First Class from 4, Special Class from 6.
  On top of a merit, the Combat Hero Medal in three classes.
- Navy and Marine Corps decorations now work like the Air Force ones: a
  sortie earns the highest decoration it qualifies for.
- The Navy's flag ranks are Rear Admiral (LH) and Rear Admiral (UH).

NEW IN THE SERVICE RECORD

- Every career card on the start page carries a briefing: your next
  promotion, aircraft ready, promotions and awards due, the store that
  runs out first, the next aircraft back from repair.
- The Army of Occupation Medal can be chosen as a World War II medal -
  contributed by Héctor, with thanks. Navy and Marine pilots can now
  choose their World War II medals too.
- The nameplate in the shadowbox always fits its frame: the rank is
  abbreviated, the first name shortened if need be, the unit set smaller.
- The Russian nomination sheets are typed at a readable size, and their
  figures read correctly ("41 боевой вылет").

FIXES

- Career Helper, custom pilot photo: "Restore original photo" could fail
  after an earlier photo. Fixed, and the tab now shows the portrait the
  career uses.
