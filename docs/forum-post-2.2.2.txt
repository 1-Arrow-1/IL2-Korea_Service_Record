IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.2.2 — your own pilot photograph


Gentlemen,

This release adds something rather more personal than another table of
figures: your own photograph can now follow your pilot through the career
screens. It also brings the Second World War service described in the game's
biographies into the Service Record, gives all three nations their own rebuilt
display cases, and fixes one remaining problem in the napalm correction.


YOUR OWN PILOT PHOTOGRAPH

The Career Helper has a new Custom pilot photo page. Give it an ordinary JPG
or PNG and it will:

- remove the background locally on your own computer;
- place you on the same photographic background as the game's portraits;
- let you drag and zoom the portrait and clean up its edge;
- adjust exposure, contrast and saturation;
- add an optional sepia treatment at any strength; and
- make the BC7 DDS texture the game expects and attach it only to the pilot in
  the selected career.

No Photoshop and no DDS exporter are required. The background-removal model
and texture converter are included, and the photograph is never uploaded
anywhere.

The game normally uses fixed sheets of small portraits on some pages. The mod
now makes those pages read the pilot's DDS directly, so the custom picture
appears in the formation sheet and event pages as well as the large, medium
and small portrait views.

Applying or restoring a picture takes a career backup first. Restore original
photo puts back the exact portrait path the career had before. Close IL-2
Korea before either operation, as you would before any Career Helper change.


THE WAR BEFORE KOREA

Many of the selectable American and Soviet biographies describe combat in the
Second World War, but the Korean career begins with none of that service on
the uniform. The Service Record can now derive the campaign decorations that
the biography itself supports.

For American pilots these include the American Defense Service Medal, the
Asiatic-Pacific and European-African-Middle Eastern Campaign Medals with the
campaign stars supported by named operations, and the World War II Victory
Medal. Soviet pilots may receive the medals for the Defense of Leningrad or
Stalingrad and Victory over Germany or Japan where their biographies say so.

They appear in the ribbon rack, on full dress and in the display case, including
careers already in progress. They are shown as prior service, without inventing
Korean War award dates or inserting tracker-only rows into the career file or
the game's own awards screen.


NEW DISPLAY CASES

The USAF, Soviet and North Korean records now each use their own frame and
glass. The wider American and Soviet cases make room for the prior-service
medals without shrinking them into a pile; the North Korean case uses its own
smaller proportions instead of borrowing the American furniture.

The brass plate now carries the squadron beneath the pilot's rank and name.
Crowded ribbon and medal groups have been rebalanced, and Save picture writes
a full-resolution JPEG. On a typical case that reduces the saved picture from
about 7.1 MB to 0.9 MB without changing its dimensions.


NAPALM CORRECTION

The automatic correction no longer pins an affected pilot's PCP to one
lifetime value. It keeps an incremental checkpoint and applies only the new
napalm excess, so a manual PCP adjustment remains in place. It still handles
the game's later writeback of the uncorrected value, and Undo preserves any
manual offset made after the first correction.

The correction also fails closed if a future game update changes the shape of
the kill-category data, and sortie recovery now logs an unexpected formation
mismatch instead of silently dropping rows.


ALSO

- Aircraft-report and evading event records introduced by the September game
  update are now identified. They are deliberately kept out of Other
  Incidences because they describe aircraft state and temporary evasion, not
  separate events in a pilot's service history.
- Custom photographs work in all known career portrait views without editing
  the game's fixed small-photo atlases.
