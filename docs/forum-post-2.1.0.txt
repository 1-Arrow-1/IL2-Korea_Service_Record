IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.1.0 — one medal per sortie


Gentlemen,

This one is mostly about the awards mod, and it changes how medals are given
out in careers you are already flying, not only in new ones. If you use the
mod, read the first two sections before you install.


ONE MEDAL PER SORTIE

Until now a single sortie could pay you several decorations at once. Shoot
down two aircraft and you might collect a Silver Star and, in the same
debrief, be walked up two or three rungs of the Air Medal — each rung
awarded and then immediately retired by the one above it, so the only trace
was a ribbon that had jumped three places while you watched.

That is finished. The decorations are now ranked in the order of precedence
the Air Force actually used — Medal of Honor, Distinguished Service Cross,
Silver Star, Legion of Merit, Distinguished Flying Cross, Soldier's Medal,
Bronze Star, Air Medal, Commendation — and a sortie pays the highest one it
earns. The others wait their turn.

So a four-kill sortie is a Distinguished Service Cross and nothing else. The
Air Medal rung you also qualified for arrives on a later flight, which is how
it should have read all along.

The awards that are not decorations for the sortie — your wings, the Purple
Heart, the Korean Service Medal, the unit citations — are untouched and still
arrive whenever they are due.


FIGHTER-BOMBER PILOTS NO LONGER HAVE TO BE LUCKY

The Silver Star and the DSC have always needed the citation to be approved as
well as earned: a 60 or 80 per cent roll on top of the kills. That is right
for a fighter squadron, where meeting aircraft is the job. It is not right
for a fighter-bomber squadron, and the game's own data says so plainly.

I went through 418 sorties flown by the pilots of a Mustang fighter-bomber
squadron. Exactly one of them produced two airborne kills. Not one produced
three or more. The reason is in the mission tables: a Mustang fighter-bomber
unit is given no interception work at all, while a Sabre wing flies a fifth
of its missions as intercepts. The two are not doing the same job.

So in a fighter-bomber squadron the roll is waived. The kill thresholds are
unchanged — two or three for the Silver Star, four or five for the DSC — but
a pilot who gets there is not then asked to win a lottery as well. Fighter
squadrons roll exactly as before.

This applies to every USAF fighter-bomber squadron in the game, and it keeps
applying to a unit that later re-equips with Sabres. That is deliberate: the
squadron is still a fighter-bomber squadron and you joined it as one.


THE DISTINGUISHED FLYING CROSS MUST NOW BE FLOWN FOR

In the stock game the DFC is handed out by the daily roster pass, on career
totals, without reference to any particular flight. It is a valour award and
it should be earned in the air, so all six rungs are now granted only by
flying. The Medal of Honor, the DSC and the Silver Star already worked this
way.

The Bronze Star with the "V" device and the Soldier's Medal stay as they are,
and that is not an oversight. The "V" is for valour on the ground and
expressly not in the air, and the Soldier's Medal is for heroism with no
enemy in it at all — a man pulled from a burning aircraft. The game models
neither act, so neither condition can look for one.


THE DISPLAY CASE AND THE RIBBON RACK

Three things were quietly wrong.

A pilot who had been awarded the Medal of Honor twice wore the plain ribbon:
the rack was showing the first rung of each ladder instead of the highest, so
the oak leaf cluster simply vanished. It now shows the highest award you
hold, on every ladder.

The awards list below it did the same in reverse — it listed every rung
separately, so "Medal of Honor" and "Medal of Honor and One Oak Leaf Cluster"
sat side by side. It now shows the highest, with the earlier ones folded
underneath it where you can still see their dates.

And the brass plate in the display case was engraving the rank in full and
then running out of room for the man, which is the wrong way round. It now
reads "2nd Lt. Meade Bennett" rather than "Second Lieutenant M. Be…". The
rank is still in the language of the air force that issued the awards, and
hovering it gives you the full rank in yours.


THE CAREER HELPER

Two fixes.

Reviving a commander now works. It restored the pilot but left the career
itself still flagged, so the game went on offering to postpone, end the
career or assign a new commander every time you loaded it.

And promotions no longer appear in the award points tab. They were sitting
there looking like medals, and handing one over did all the wrong things at
once — it raised a random skill instead of the rank, wrote the wrong kind of
record, and spent an award point for nothing. A promotion belongs to the
game, which lets you choose the attribute yourself. The helper now leaves it
alone.


STILL OUTSTANDING: THE 29 SEPTEMBER SORTIE PROBLEM

The fault I described in 2.0.0 — the game discarding the detailed record of
each flight — has been acknowledged by the developers and a fix is expected
in the next game update. As far as I know it has not shipped yet. The check
is still ten seconds:

    ...\IL2Series\data\_career.log

and search for "escapedJail". The tracker still warns you at the top of a
career the game has damaged.
