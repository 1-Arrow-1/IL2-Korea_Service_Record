IL-2 Korea Service Record 1.8.1 — signed, and the Award points tab shows its work


Gentlemen,

A small release, but one thing in it matters more than its size.


IT IS SIGNED NOW

The setup program, the tracker and the Career Helper are all Authenticode signed.
Right-click the setup program, Properties, Digital Signatures, and it will tell you
who made it.

This is why: a user found that Windows Smart App Control refused to start the Career
Helper at all, because nothing in the release was signed. He turned Smart App Control
off to get past it — and Windows will not let you turn that back on again without
reinstalling, which is a poor thing to have to spend on a hobby tool. That should not
happen to anyone else now.

SmartScreen is a separate thing from signing and works on reputation, so a brand-new
release can still raise a warning until enough people have run it. It will settle.


THE AWARD POINTS TAB SHOWS WHAT IT CHANGED

Presenting a batch of awards raises the pilots' attributes, and until now the list
simply emptied and left you to guess what had moved.

The tab now carries three attributes — skill, discipline and courage — each as a pair
of columns: what the man stood at before, and what he stands at after.

    Pilot              Award                     Skill  after   Discipline  after   Courage  after
    Hayden Burkhart    Pilot's Badge                1     ↑ 3        1       ↑ 2        2       2
    Erik Greiert       Air Medal and Three Oak…     4       4        5         5        3     ↑ 5

The right-hand column of each pair is blank until you press Present. Then the rows stay
on screen with both sides filled in and an arrow against everything that actually went
up, instead of the list clearing and telling you nothing.

Boosters are deliberately absent. A decoration never moves them — only a promotion does.


A FRONT DOOR

The career list used to be a heading and a row of cards. It now opens on a Mustang,
and the setup program carries the same picture on its welcome page instead of the
installer toolkit's stock blue panel.

Nothing else changed visually. The record, the diary and the display case are paper,
and a glossy render has no business printed on them.


TWO SMALLER FIXES

  * A very long pilot name is no longer cut off mid-word on the display case's brass.
    It shrinks to fit, and where even that will not do it ends in an ellipsis, so you
    can see that something was shortened rather than wondering.
  * In a narrow window the combat results strip clipped the word ARMAMENTS. It now
    tightens, and then wraps, rather than cutting the label short.


THE MOD

Unchanged from 1.7.0, byte for byte. Nothing to merge or redo.
