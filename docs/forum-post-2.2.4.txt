IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.2.4 — personnel files, your own biography, a squadron forecast


Gentlemen,

2.2.4 is out. It brings a few things you asked for, some I wanted myself,
and one fix that matters if you are on game update 1.004.

FIX FOR GAME UPDATE 1.004

- The mod replaces two of the game's career screens so custom portraits show
  in the game. Its copies were older than 1.004 and hid the update's own
  changes, such as the pending-awards marker. Both are now rebuilt from the
  1.004 originals, with only the portrait change made.
- If a future game update changes those screens again, the career page now
  says so instead of hiding the update quietly.

NEW: THE PERSONNEL FILE (USAF)

- One printable document per pilot: cover sheet with photo, record of
  service, his commissions, his award certificates, the biography, the
  flight record (two months to a page) and the shadowbox.
- Every promotion now comes with a commission from the President, filled in
  by hand and signed by the officials in office on that date.
- One certificate per decoration as he wears it. The earlier awards of each
  ladder are listed in the record of service with their citations.
- Print it, or choose "Save as PDF" for a file to keep or post.
- Soviet, Chinese and North Korean files will follow.

NEW IN THE CAREER HELPER: YOUR OWN BIOGRAPHY

- The game shows your biography only when you create the career. The new
  Biography tab lets you rewrite it at any time. The Service Record shows
  your version, with your name, birth date and starting rank filled in.
- Choose which World War II medals your pilot earned before Korea
  (USAF and Soviet pilots). They appear on his uniforms and in the shadowbox.
- Share out your booster points again - skill, discipline and courage - at
  most 3 per attribute plus one for every promotion. The game is not touched
  by the text or the medals; the boosters are written to the career, with a
  backup first.

NEW IN THE SERVICE RECORD

- Squadron statistics: click the + beside RANK and every pilot shows what
  he still needs for his next promotion.
- Materiel forecast: what a flying day costs the squadron in fuel, ordnance
  and equipment, what the next week, fortnight and month should cost at
  your pace, and how long your stocks last. From now on the tracker
  measures the real consumption from your stocks and uses that once it has
  three flying days.
- Medals have a proper shadow again, in the record and in the shadowbox,
  and the oak leaf clusters and stars stand off the ribbon.
- The flight record shortens long ranks (Lt. Colonel, Brig. General) so the
  GRADE box is never cut off.
- A certificate opened from the record now closes its tab when you go back,
  like the flight record and the war diary.

AWARDS MOD

- Silver Star and DSC no longer stop at a fixed number of kills. A sortie
  earns the highest decoration it qualifies for: Silver Star from 2 air
  kills, DSC from 4, Medal of Honor from 6. Once you hold the full ladder of
  one, the next one down takes its place - a pilot who already has the
  Medal of Honor with its cluster now gets a DSC for his next 6-kill sortie
  instead of nothing.
- In the F-86 squadrons the Silver Star and DSC still have to be approved:
  60% at the lower kill count, 80% above it. A failed DSC can still bring a
  Silver Star.
- These rules apply to the USAF. Navy and Marine Corps decorations work the
  old way for now.
