IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.2.0 — napalm, lost sorties, tail codes


Gentlemen,

This release deals with two game bugs that have been quietly bending career
records since the 29 September update, and it is the first version that
writes corrections into your career file. Read the first two sections before
you install.


NAPALM COUNTS THE SAME TARGET OVER AND OVER

Napalm burning on an airfield's buildings, tents, ammunition stores and parked
aircraft books the same destroyed object again every two seconds for as long
as the fire burns - about three minutes. One part, one fire: 90 kills. Several
fires on one part: 270, 450, 900. A napalm drop on a lone AA gun counts once,
correctly; it is the airfield block groups that go wrong.

In my own career one sortie destroyed 13 objects and was credited with 867.
Those kills go straight into your kill totals, your score and your PCP, and
from there into every award that counts ground targets. The hotfix 1.004b
does not change it - I tested it this evening. It is reported to the
developers.

What 2.2.0 does about it:

- The Service Record shows the real kill counts everywhere: combat results,
  the mission report, best sorties, airframes, squadron totals. It rebuilds
  them from the game's own record of every destroyed object, which is not
  affected.
- When it opens a career, it writes the corrected kill totals and PCP back
  into the career file, so the awards and promotions read honest numbers
  from your next mission on. It touches nothing unless something is
  inflated, and it takes a backup first, every time.
- The awards mod no longer pays a ground-kill award or a promotion in the
  debrief of a sortie with napalm-sized numbers (60 buildings or 60 ground
  targets). No honest sortie in six careers came near that; the inflated
  ones start at 83. If you qualify for real, it arrives on your next flight.

Your score is left as the game has it.


THE MISSIONS THE GAME FORGOT

Between the 29 September update and the hotfix 1.004b of 2 October, the game
saved no sortie details at all. The missions were there, the awards and
totals moved, but the debriefings stayed empty. Install 1.004b and it stops -
and the patch script I handed out is no longer needed; please do not run it
again.

The missions you already lost come back. The game kept the full debrief of
every mission, and 2.2.0 rebuilds the missing sortie rows from it the first
time it opens the career. I checked the method against 701 sorties that were
saved normally: every field matched on every one.

Hector (hjbb1975) worked out the same thing independently and recovered 96
sorties of his own. Thank you, Hector.


ALSO

- Tail codes: the F-86 shows FU-174 instead of BN-174, and F-84E codes decode
  at all. Both fixes are Hector's.
- Promotions: the PCP needed above Captain is lower - 250, 400, 650, 1000 and
  1500 - set after the napalm points came out of everyone's PCP.


IF YOU WANT TO UNDO IT

Every write is backed up first to
%LOCALAPPDATA%\IL2KoreaTracker\backups, next to a record of every original
value. Nothing is lost.
