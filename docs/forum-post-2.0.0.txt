IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.0.0 — and a warning about the 29 September game update


Gentlemen,

Read the first section even if you never touch the tracker. It concerns your
career files.


THE GAME UPDATE IS THROWING AWAY YOUR SORTIES

Since the 29 September update, IL-2 Korea does not save the record of the
individual flights in a career. The mission is saved. Your awards are saved.
Your sortie counter goes up. But the detailed flight records underneath —
who flew, for how long, what they hit — are discarded before they reach the
career file.

Nothing looks wrong in game. You fly, you debrief, you collect a medal, the
numbers move. It is silent.

You can check it in ten seconds. Open

    ...\IL2Series\data\_career.log

and search for "escapedJail". If you find lines like

    [SQL ERROR] sqlSave; 1; table sortie has no column named escapedJail

then that is it — one line per pilot per mission. Those are your sorties
failing to save.

It affects everyone on this build, including careers started after it. It is
not something about your installation, and it is not caused by any mod. I
traced it to a single mistaken column name in one of the game's own database
statements and reported it to the developers with a reproduction. It is a
small mistake with a small fix, so I hope it comes back quickly.

Until it does: do not fly a career you care about. The missions already
affected cannot be recovered — the data was never written. Your career file
is not corrupted and nothing else is at risk: awards, promotions, the roster,
aircraft and operations are all fine.


SO THE RECORD NOW TELLS YOU

Several of you reported the tracker as broken, which was fair — a flight
record with holes in it and hours that disagree with the game looks exactly
like a broken tracker.

Open a career the game has damaged and the record now says so at the top, in
plain words, with the number of affected missions and the date they start
from. It will not blame itself for the game's mistake, and it will not blame
the game when the fault is its own: a mission you have not flown yet is not
counted.


2.0.0 READS THE NEW BUILD AT ALL

This is why the version number jumps.

The update changed how the game stores its text and artwork, and 1.8.1 simply
cannot read any of it on the current build. Award names, rank names, mission
names — all of it would come back blank or as raw codes. 2.0.0 reads both the
old and the new format and works out which your installation has, so it runs
correctly whether or not you have updated.

While fixing that I found a second, older fault: the tracker kept its own
copy of the game's text and never noticed when the game changed it. On this
machine it was serving names that were eighteen days stale. It now refreshes
whenever the game is patched — which also means the two new mission types,
Strategic Object Cover and Enemy Harbour Attack, appear by name rather than
as a number.


OPERATIONS

The squadron statistics now show how many operations the unit has seen
through and how many it won:

    OPERATIONS    8 (5 successful)

Be warned that this is harsher than it looks. An operation your squadron flew
nothing in still counts against the total — that is the game's own scoring,
not mine, and it is why a good unit can sit at five from eight.

The war diary now says which way each one went, instead of closing every
operation with the same line. And eastern careers no longer print "EB" in
front of every operation name, which was my mistake: it means Eastern Bloc.


THE PAGES HAVE BEEN DRESSED

Alex has been drawing.

The front door now shows a different aircraft each time you open it — six of
them, from the Mustang to the MiG-15 — standing on a faint briefing table.
The service record sits on a restricted navigation chart, the war diary on an
operations map, and the flight record on a flight line with a Mustang and its
ground crew. All of them are turned well down and none of them print.

The flight record is also signed now, in a period hand, above the line where
the typed name sits. Russian and Chinese pilots get a signature in their own
script rather than a block of sans-serif.

If the chart behind the service record is not to your taste there is a small
switch beside the language picker to turn it off. It is remembered.


THE MOD

Unchanged. The 29 September update altered several award conditions on the
game's side and I am working through what that means for the mod, which will
follow separately once I have tested it rather than guessed.
