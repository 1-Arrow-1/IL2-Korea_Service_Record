IL-2 Korea Service Record 2.2.3 — hotfix for custom portraits


Gentlemen,

If you are on 2.2.2 and gave your pilot a custom portrait with the Career
Helper, please update now.

THE PROBLEM

The game only accepts a portrait name of up to 15 characters. 2.2.2 wrote a
longer one, and from then on the game crashed to the desktop every time the
career moved on to a new day.

THE FIX

- 2.2.3 gives custom portraits a short name the game can handle.
- If your career is already affected, open it once in the Service Record
  after updating. It moves your portrait to the short name and repairs the
  career automatically, with a backup first. Your portrait stays.
- After that, the next day works again. Nothing else in your career changes.

ALSO

- The pilot biography now sits on a desk instead of a plain background.
- A note on 2.2.0's napalm correction: the repeated kills come from any fire
  on airfield buildings, not only napalm - a bomb that sets a fuel store
  burning does it too. The correction already handles both.
