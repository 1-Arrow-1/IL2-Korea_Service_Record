A second award of the Medal of Honor is denoted, in Army and Air Force practice, by a bronze oak leaf cluster worn on the ribbon; the naval services use a gold star for the same purpose.

Nineteen men have received the Medal of Honor twice. Five of them were given both the Army and the Navy medal for the same action, and all such double awards date from before 1920. The legislation and regulations that followed the First World War made repetition effectively impossible, and no second award has been made since.

Four United States Air Force airmen received the Medal of Honor during the Korean War, every one of them posthumously. No airman has ever been decorated with it twice.
