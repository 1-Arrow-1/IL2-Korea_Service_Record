The Chinese People's Volunteers recorded a deed as merit rather than marking it with a medal. The unit's merit committee weighed the deed, entered it in the soldier's merit booklet, the Certificate of Meritorious Service, and sent glad tidings of it to his family at home. There were four grades: Third, Second and First Class merit and, above them, Special Class merit. A fighter pilot's merit was most often recorded for aircraft shot down.

First Class merit was recorded for an outstanding deed and was rare.
