Awarded for heroism not involving actual conflict with an armed enemy. Established by Act of Congress on 2 July 1926. Until the Airman's Medal was created in 1960 it was the decoration the Air Force gave its own men for such acts: a comrade pulled from a burning aircraft, a rescue from the sea, a life saved at the voluntary risk of one's own. It is worn after the Distinguished Flying Cross and before the Bronze Star.
